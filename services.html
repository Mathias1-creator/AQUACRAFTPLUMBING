Our plumbing services

AQUACRAFT PLUMBING INC. handles residential and light commercial plumbing across Sonoma, Napa, and Marin counties — anything with water, sewer, or gas. Here's a closer look at what we do.

Drain Cleaning & Clog Removal

Sinks, tubs, showers, and main lines — we clear clogs and slow drains and get water moving again. We work cleanly and double-check that the blockage is fully resolved before we call the job done.

Water Heater Repair & Installation (Tank & Tankless)

Whether you have a traditional tank or a tankless system, we repair, service, and install water heaters so you always have reliable hot water. When it's time to replace, we'll walk you through the right option for your home or business.

Plumbing Fixture Installation & Repair

Faucets, sinks, toilets, valves, and more — installed and repaired correctly the first time. New fixtures are fitted to look right and work right, and existing ones are restored to proper function.

Water Leak Detection & Repair

Leaks can hide behind walls and under floors. We locate the source, explain what we find, and make the repair — protecting your property and keeping your water bill in check.

Sewer Service

From slow-draining lines to bigger sewer issues, we diagnose the problem and provide the service needed to keep wastewater flowing safely away from your property.

Gas Line Service

Gas work demands care. We provide safe, code-conscious gas line service for appliances, water heaters, and more — done right and tested for your peace of mind.

Emergency Plumbing (24/7)

Burst pipe at midnight? Water where it shouldn't be? We're available around the clock. Call or text and we'll respond to your plumbing emergency, day or night.
