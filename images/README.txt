AQUACRAFT PLUMBING INC. — site photos
=====================================

Drop royalty-free photos in THIS folder using these exact names and the site
picks them up automatically (no code changes). Until a file is present, that
section shows a clean branded gradient instead — never a broken image.

Files the site looks for:

1. work.jpg   — Home page "Careful, quality workmanship" band (full-width).
               Best: a wide LANDSCAPE photo, ~1600x900px or larger.
               Subject ideas: a plumber working, copper pipes, tools,
               a clean install, a service van.

2. about.jpg  — About page image panel (next to the company story).
               Best: PORTRAIT or square, ~1000x1100px.
               Subject ideas: plumber at work, tools laid out, a tidy
               finished job, a friendly tradesperson.

Where to get free, commercial-use photos (search in your browser):
  - Unsplash: https://unsplash.com/s/photos/plumber   (free, no attribution)
  - Pexels:   https://www.pexels.com/search/plumber/   (free, no attribution)

Two ways to add them:
  A) Commit the files here as images/work.jpg and images/about.jpg, OR
  B) Upload them to your Google Drive and ask Claude to pull them in — they'll
     be optimized, placed, and verified for you (same as the logo was).

Keep files reasonably small (ideally under ~400 KB each) so pages load fast.
